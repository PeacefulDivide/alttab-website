An eye-care utility for light sensitivity and night use. Dim your AMOLED screen below the system minimum, without flicker.

Features
  • Sub-pixel mesh overlay that dims AMOLED screens beyond system limits, with no flicker
  • Active burn-in protection with pixel-shifting every 2 minutes
  • Quick intensity toggles right in the status bar notification
  • Naturally cuts power draw on AMOLED displays by filtering pixels
  • Privacy-first: zero telemetry, and the free app is 100% offline
