Kept: Private Home Inventory

Organize what you own. Scan it, photograph it, track its warranty, and keep all of it on your own phone.

Get Free on Google Play

See it in action

The problem it solves

Whether it is a collection you love, the contents of your home for insurance, or the gear in your workshop, the details end up scattered across receipts, photos and memory. Kept puts it all in one place, and the place is your own phone.

Features
  • Organize items into collections, such as rooms, hobbies, tools or anything you like
  • Photos for every collection and item, with a list or photo grid view
  • Scan barcodes to add items and to search, including serial numbers, all on the device
  • Track model, serial number, price paid, purchase date, store and warranty
  • Reminders before a warranty ends or an item expires
  • Wishlist and favorites, with quick filters
  • Dashboard with item count, estimated value and total paid
  • Import and export spreadsheets (CSV), so your data is never locked in
  • App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
  • Undo after deleting an item or a whole collection

Private by design

Your inventory never leaves your phone. Kept has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Barcodes are read on the device with open-source software, and the only network use is Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase. Read the privacy policy.
