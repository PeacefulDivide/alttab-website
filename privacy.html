Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

AltTab Technologies LLC ("we", "us") builds LumaLens, LumaLens Pro, Shutter Shade and PadMute. This policy explains what these apps and this website do with your information. In short: we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.

Our apps

  • None of our apps require an account.
  • None of our apps contain advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Settings are stored only on your own device.
  • We do not receive your settings, usage, or any other data from the apps.
  • Each app only uses the system permissions it needs to work, described below.

PadMute (Windows)

  • Microphone access. PadMute uses the Windows microphone permission only to mute and unmute your capture devices and to tell whether a microphone is in use. It never records, listens to, stores or transmits audio.
  • Game controllers. PadMute reads button presses from connected controllers only to detect your mute shortcut. It never writes to a controller and does not store or transmit button data.
  • Keyboard hotkey. The hotkey is registered with Windows. PadMute does not log keystrokes.
  • Pro purchase. The optional Pro upgrade is bought and verified through the Microsoft Store. Payment is handled entirely by Microsoft; we never see your payment details. PadMute only checks whether the license is active.
  • PadMute makes no network requests of its own, other than the Microsoft Store license check.

LumaLens and LumaLens Pro Unlocker (Android)

  • We do not collect, transmit or store personal data, usage statistics or device identifiers.
  • Accessibility Service. Used only to draw the true black sub-pixel mesh overlay on top of everything on screen. It does not read screen content or monitor how you interact with apps.
  • Display over other apps. Used to draw the dimming filter over the screen.
  • Notifications and battery optimization. Used for the status notification with toggles, and to keep the pixel-shifting routine running.
  • The free app is fully offline. The Pro Unlocker uses the internet and Google Play Billing only for purchase and license verification and the optional tip jar. Payments are handled by Google; we never see your payment details.

Full details: LumaLens privacy policy.

Shutter Shade (Windows)

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information or usage analytics, and the app has no telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • To pause or restore the filter for programs on your exclusion list, the app reads the titles and process names of running windows. This stays in memory and is never saved or transmitted.
  • Your settings are stored locally on your PC.
  • The only online activity is the Microsoft Store license and purchase check, handled by Microsoft.

Full details: Shutter Shade privacy policy.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies or analytics on it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you.

Children

Our apps are general-purpose utilities and do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app store listings.
