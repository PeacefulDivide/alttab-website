Shutter Shade

A lightweight Windows night light that gets out of the way. Blue light reduction on by default, and off automatically for the apps where color matters.

Get Free on Microsoft Store

See it in action

Features
  • Blue light reduction on by default, adjustable with a simple slider
  • Automatically restores display color accuracy for the programs you select (up to 5 apps free, unlimited with Pro)
  • Scan for installed games, or add any running app to the exclusion list in one click
  • Quick access from the system tray, and starts with Windows if you want
  • Native Win32 C++ with minimal system usage
  • Privacy-first: zero data collection, tracking or telemetry

Free and Pro

Everything is included for free. The only difference is how many apps you can exclude from the filter.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. To pause the filter for the apps on your exclusion list, Shutter Shade reads the titles and process names of running windows. That stays in memory and is never saved or sent anywhere. Shutter Shade privacy policy • Company privacy policy
